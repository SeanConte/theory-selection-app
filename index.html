Who's the hidden creature?
A creature has been secretly selected from a board of candidates. Ask questions to eliminate the impossible ones — and identify the target.

How it works: You'll see a board of creatures, each with a unique combination of visible properties. One has been chosen. Ask questions about its properties to rule out creatures that don't match.

There's no hard limit — but the fewer questions you use, the higher your score. Think carefully: which question would eliminate the most creatures at once?

Something is spreading.
A disease has broken out in the community. Six candidate diseases are on the table. Observe the outbreak, test individuals, and place quarantines to figure out which one it is.

How it works: Community members move around a shared space. When they come close to someone infected, there's a chance of transmission. You'll observe who develops symptoms over time.

You have a limited action budget — use it for tests (reveal true infection state) and quarantines (block a region). When you're confident, submit a diagnosis.

No time limit. The fewer actions you use, the higher your score.

🌱 How to use the garden

Select plots: Click and drag to select a region. Selected plots glow green.

Assign conditions: A popup appears after you select. Set one or more conditions — leave others as "no change" to update only specific variables.

Run a day: Once every plot is initialized, click Run Day. Not every variable has the same shape: some have a sharp optimum, others are flatter, and some seeds include interactions.

Hover any plot to see its day-by-day history.
